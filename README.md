## About me

I am a statistician with hands-on experience in survey research, socio-economic data and retail panel data.
I enjoy building reproducible analytics with the tidyverse, designing compact KPI dashboards, and writing clean,
well-documented R code that supports meaningful research.

- **Now:** teaching statistics at AIBA Sylhet (affiliated with BUP)
- **Previously:** Analysis Executive at Kantar Bangladesh and Luminaries Research; Research Assistant, Department of Statistics, University of Chittagong
- **Building:** Shiny apps and R tooling that automate survey and panel-data reporting
- **Open to:** collaboration on R packages, Shiny apps and survey-research projects
- **Ask me about:** statistics, R, data cleaning and reproducible reporting
